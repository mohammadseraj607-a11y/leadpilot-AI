Everything a lead needs, from first message to signed deal

One workspace for the people you sell to, the work you owe them, and the numbers that show what's working.

  • Intelligent lead management

    Capture leads from forms, CSV imports or by hand. Duplicates are caught before they clutter your list.

  • AI-powered customer insights

    Summaries, stated needs, open questions and blockers, with customer facts kept apart from AI estimates.

  • Automated follow-ups

    Rules create follow-up tasks and reminders the moment a lead arrives or a deal reaches proposal.

  • Sales pipeline management

    Drag deals between stages or use the stage picker. Every move is saved to the deal history.

  • AI email generation

    Draft a follow-up in your chosen tone, edit it, then send through your own email provider when you approve.

  • Actionable analytics

    Conversion, win rate, weighted pipeline and team performance, calculated from recorded data only.
